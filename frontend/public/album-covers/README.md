# Record sleeves

The collection uses real release artwork, downloaded at 400 × 400 from the
Apple Music catalogue. These are sleeve images, not newly generated artwork.

- `stand-by-me.jpg`: Ben E. King, *Don't Play That Song!* — [catalogue entry](https://music.apple.com/us/album/stand-by-me/68211636?i=68210790).
- `nothing-can-change-this-love.jpg`: Sam Cooke, *Mr. Soul* — [catalogue entry](https://music.apple.com/us/album/nothing-can-change-this-love/497769621?i=497769795).
- `come-a-little-bit-closer.jpg`: Jay & The Americans, *Come a Little Bit Closer* — [catalogue entry](https://music.apple.com/us/album/come-a-little-bit-closer/1376374519?i=1376375100).

Artwork remains the property of its respective rights holders. The existing
`family-business.png` is retained from the earlier local demo.
